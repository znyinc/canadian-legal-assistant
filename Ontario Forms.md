Here is a concise “Ontario legal forms starter pack” with the main official hubs you can bookmark and search when you need something specific. [ontariocourtforms.on](https://ontariocourtforms.on.ca/en/)

## Master form hubs

- **Ontario Court Forms (all courts):** centralized entry point for Family, Civil, and Small Claims court forms. [ontariocourtforms.on](https://ontariocourtforms.on.ca/en/)
- **Central Forms Repository (CFR):** general Ontario government forms, including employment, tenancy, business, health, etc.. [forms.mgcs.gov.on](https://forms.mgcs.gov.on.ca)
- **Ontario Court rules & practice:** rules and some practice directions for Superior Court (civil/family)  and step‑by‑step family case info. [ontariocourtforms.on](https://ontariocourtforms.on.ca/en/rules-of-civil-procedure-forms/)

## Family law, divorce, custody

- **Family Law Rules forms (general family court):** all forms under the Family Law Rules (e.g., Form 8 – Application, Form 13 – Financial Statement, Form 35.1 – Parenting affidavit). [ontariocourtforms.on](https://ontariocourtforms.on.ca/en/family-law-rules-forms/)
- **Family Law Act forms:** forms used specifically under the Family Law Act (support, restraining orders, etc.). [ontariocourtforms.on](https://ontariocourtforms.on.ca/en/family-law-act-forms/)
- **Online filing – family court:** file divorce, parenting, support, and property documents online where permitted. [ontario](http://www.ontario.ca/page/file-family-court-documents-online)
- **Guided Pathways – family law (CLEO):** Q&A style tool that fills in the correct family court forms for you. [stepstojustice](https://stepstojustice.ca/guided-pathways/family-law-about/)

## Civil and small claims

- **Rules of Civil Procedure forms (Superior Court – civil):** all forms for regular civil actions (claims, motions, affidavits, judgments, appeals, etc.). [ontariocourtforms.on](https://ontariocourtforms.on.ca/en/rules-of-civil-procedure-forms/)
- **Small Claims Court forms:** all forms for claims up to the small claims limit (e.g., Plaintiff’s Claim 7A, Default Judgment 11B, Summons to Witness 18A) in PDF and fillable Word. [ontariocourtforms.on](https://ontariocourtforms.on.ca/en/rules-of-the-small-claims-court-forms/)
- **Online filing – Small Claims Court:** Small Claims Court Guided Pathways and e‑filing for a lot of standard small claims actions. [ontario](https://www.ontario.ca/page/file-small-claims-court-documents-online)

## Landlord–tenant and housing

- **Landlord and Tenant Board (LTB) main page:** overview of the board, choosing and filing applications. [tribunalsontario](https://tribunalsontario.ca/ltb/)
- **LTB forms, filing and fees:** all L, T, and N series forms (L1–L5, T1–T7, notices of termination, fee waiver, affidavit, certificate of service, etc.) + how to file and pay. [tribunalsontario](https://tribunalsontario.ca/ltb/forms-filing-and-fees/)
- **Tribunals Ontario Portal (for LTB e‑filing):** online portal to start and manage LTB applications; PDFs are still available beside the portal. [tribunalsontario](https://tribunalsontario.ca/ltb/tribunals-ontario-portal/)
- **Standard residential lease (Ontario):** mandatory residential tenancy agreement (standard lease) form for most private rentals. [forms.mgcs.gov.on](https://forms.mgcs.gov.on.ca/en/dataset/047-2229)

## Employment, business, and general government

- **Employment Standards / Ministry of Labour forms:** many ESA‑related forms are in CFR under the Ministry of Labour (e.g., extended temporary layoff requests). [forms.mgcs.gov.on](https://forms.mgcs.gov.on.ca/en/dataset/?q=014-3296-64E&_organization_limit=0&organization=ministry-of-labour-immigration-training-and-skills-development&page=7)
- **Other ministry‑specific forms:** search CFR by ministry or keyword (e.g., “driver’s license”, “health”, “business registration”) to reach the right program form set. [forms.mgcs.gov.on](https://forms.mgcs.gov.on.ca)

## Wills, powers of attorney, estates

- Ontario does not have a single “official will form,” but there are standardized **Powers of Attorney** forms and many public guides.  
- **Guides and Pathways for wills/POA:** community legal education and guided tools (often linked from CLEO / Steps to Justice or local legal clinics) help generate valid Powers of Attorney and simple wills for Ontario residents. [youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2qYV6nzFkwo)

## How to use this in practice

- Start with **Ontario Court Forms** when it’s anything court‑based (family, civil, small claims) and then drill down to the correct rules set. [ontariocourtforms.on](https://ontariocourtforms.on.ca/en/)
- Start with **LTB forms** + Tribunals Ontario Portal for any residential landlord–tenant issue, and with the **standard lease** when starting a new tenancy. [tribunalsontario](https://tribunalsontario.ca/ltb/forms-filing-and-fees/)
- Start with the **Central Forms Repository** for everything else government‑related (employment, business, health, transportation, etc.). [forms.mgcs.gov.on](https://forms.mgcs.gov.on.ca)
